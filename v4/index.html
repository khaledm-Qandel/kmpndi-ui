kmpndi turns your compound's map (zones, buildings, units, owners) into live gate access. Residents issue their own QR passes. Guards scan. You stop managing lists.

Your gate still runs on paper.

Guest lists on WhatsApp. Worker cards nobody checks. Tenants who moved out months ago and still wave their way in. The guard does their best with what they're given, and what they're given is guesswork.

  • Unreadable

    A blurry photo of an ID card

    Forwarded three times on WhatsApp. The guard can't tell whose guest it is.

  • Expired

    A laminated card from last year

    The contract ended, but the card still gets a nod at the gate.

  • Revoked?

    A face the guard remembers

    The lease ended in spring. Nobody told the gate.

  • Unknown

    Three calls to three villas

    Nobody answers, the order waits at the barrier, and the resident is angry.

kmpndi replaces the guesswork with one rule the gate can check: who owns or lives in the unit, and who they said may come in.

    Sell a unit. The keys change hands.

    Access in kmpndi comes from ownership and occupancy, recorded like a title deed. When a unit is sold or leased, the right people gain access and the wrong people lose it, automatically. Nobody has to remember to update the gate.

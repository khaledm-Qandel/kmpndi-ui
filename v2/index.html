    One pass for every kind of visit.

    Residents create them in the app and send them on WhatsApp. Every pass comes from the resident's own right, so it can never outlast it. The gate checks the window, not the guard's memory.

    The last three are what the gate sees when a pass isn't good.

    Sell a unit. The keys change hands.

    Access in kmpndi comes from ownership and occupancy, recorded like a title deed. When a unit is sold or leased, the right people gain access and the wrong people lose it, automatically. Nobody has to remember to update the gate.
